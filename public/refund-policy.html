Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Customer cancellation

Customers may request cancellation while an order is new or accepted. Cancellation may not be available after preparation has started. The cancellation button and the restaurant’s decision determine the final order status.

Restaurant cancellation

The restaurant may cancel an order when it is closed, an item is unavailable, the address is outside the delivery area, or the order cannot reasonably be fulfilled.

Cash orders

Cash on Delivery orders do not require a payment refund. If the order is cancelled before delivery, no cash should be collected.

Online payments

Online payments are not currently enabled. When enabled, approved refunds will be returned through the original payment provider and may take additional time to appear in the customer’s account.

Damaged, missing, or incorrect orders

Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] within [X HOURS] of delivery with the order number and details. We may request photographs or other information and will review each case fairly.
